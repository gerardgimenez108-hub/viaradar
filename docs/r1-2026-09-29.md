## Improvement boundary

- Keep strict stopped-publication evaluation separate from an experimental published-assignment target.
- Add a repeatable read-only stability/readiness report to reveal coverage and variability instead of promising a waiting period.
- Capture immutable schedule/context for future services before evaluating hour/day/service-aware candidates; a freshly downloaded static timetable must not silently rewrite historical features.
- Evaluate future dates without later-publication leakage, and report both coverage and agreement. No estimate is preferable to directing passengers to a track supported by only 20% of similar services.
- Predicting a platform and predicting which physical train will depart first are different problems. Neither a timetable time nor a door-closing signal is a guaranteed departure time in this app.

No R1 candidate is promoted by this work. There is no defensible fixed number of days until public R1 predictions: the current engine is mismatched to the available R1 evidence, and a contextual candidate still needs validation.
